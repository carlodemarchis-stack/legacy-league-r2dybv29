Chicago lead Round 2 at Mayakoba by four. At 14:20 local time they are −23 for the event, six under for the day with ten holes complete. Captain Isaiah Castellano's eagle three at the par-five 7th is the biggest single gain, and Maya Stafford has supplied five of the team's twenty counted scores. Andre Ingram already has an eagle at the 13th, a hole Chicago have not yet completed as a team.

Atlanta are the movers. Seven under through 11 holes is the best round on the course so far and has taken them from joint sixth overnight to a share of second at −19. It started with Jordan Baptiste's eagle two at the par-four 3rd, and Lamar Jarrett has counted on nine of the 11 holes.

Dallas are level with Atlanta, five under through ten, with captain Victor Ingram counting on seven of those holes. Boston, Philadelphia and San Francisco share fourth at −18. Boston got there with the shot of the day: Andre Valdez holed a tee shot at the 200-yard 10th at 13:28.

Five cities are within five shots of the lead and all have at least eight holes left. If it finished like this, Chicago would take 100 league points, Atlanta and Dallas 72.5 each, and the three cities in fourth 46 each. The last group is due in at 16:29.
