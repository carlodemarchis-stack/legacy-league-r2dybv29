Five cities finished round one inside four shots of each other, which is roughly what the format promised. Dallas led after a steady day in which seven players contributed at least one counted ball, and nobody posted worse than a bogey on a counted hole.

Chicago were the movers. A back nine of nine under, built on three birdies in a row from the 14th, took them from the middle of the board to second. Miami's two lost balls on 16, both to the same gust, cost them a counted double and the chance of a top-five start.
