Each city's team plays for one charity, named by the players, working on mental health in that city. A fixed share of the league points a team scores converts into funding at the end of the season, and every event adds a city-specific grant on top.

The eleven partners are listed on the Mission page with what the money does. Donations from fans open later in the season.
