Every city fields six to eight players. On each hole, the best two scores among them count toward the team total; the others are discarded. Two rounds make an event, and the event finishing positions convert to league points on a fixed table.

Ties share the points on offer. The season champion is the city with the most points after five events; the Team Championship in October is played for a separate purse.
